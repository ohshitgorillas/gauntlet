3. The extras are the author's and not the reviewer's. Hold one probe, `printf x > <tmp>/outside/probe.txt`, and one declaration, `extra_binds: ["<tmp>/outside"]`, and move the caller: under `prosecutor` the file is on disk after the run, under `arbiter` it is not.
   kills: an implementation emitting the extras in every profile hands a blind reviewer a writable path outside the checkout, so the `arbiter` writes `<tmp>/outside/probe.txt` exactly as the author does.

Surface under test: the wire surface of the PreToolUse lane hooks in .claude/hooks/. One JSON object on stdin carrying `tool_name`, `tool_input` and `cwd`, and, where a line says so, `agent_type`. The hook answers on stdout with either nothing at all, written SILENT below, which is the call being allowed, or a JSON object whose `hookSpecificOutput.permissionDecision` is `deny`, written DENY below. The hooks are invoked as `python3 .claude/hooks/<hook>.py` with the payload on stdin. Every payload carries `tool_name` `Bash`, `tool_input` `{"command": <the command>}` and cwd /home/atom/dev/gauntlet, and no `agent_type` key unless the line names one.

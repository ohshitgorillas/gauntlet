## What is wrong

Reviewer output is the largest per-round artifact the chain makes. `gauntlet/reviews/` holds 109 plan rounds over 23 slugs, 853,223 bytes, longest run 11 rounds on one slug — 7.8KB mean per round, this plan's own rounds excluded. Owner's table reads 23 plans and 11 max rounds; mean off disk is 4.7, not 4.4.

Two shapes in `agents/prosecutor.md` make the bulk, and neither carries a finding.

`agents/prosecutor.md:103` — `a  PASS  <the escape the plan took, and where you resolved it>`. No cap, so a PASS pays a paragraph. Four PASS lines in `gauntlet/reviews/triviajudge-extract.plan.1.txt` run 2,308 bytes.

Check (b), `agents/prosecutor.md:53` — "Open each citation, compare against what plan says it shows". Reading instruction, not a printing one; the uncapped shape at `:103` turns it into printing. Sample's `b` line opens with 1,241 bytes reciting citations that resolved — `gauntlet/reviews/triviajudge-extract.plan.1.txt:30`, "I opened every cited line and all 28 symbol citations" — before naming three that do not.

`agents/arbiter.md:197` — `N  ADMITTED  <input> -> <outcome>; <kills: implementation> fails it at <input>; bite: <measured value at HEAD> | null stub fails at <input>`. Every field copied out of the behavior line the arbiter was handed. Arbiter writes that block to `<gauntlet dir>/specs/approved/<slug>.txt` on `READY` (`agents/arbiter.md:57`), so an `ADMITTED` line restates a file the same agent lands.

Each round is paid four times: written to the round file (`agents/prosecutor.md:37`), returned to the main agent, re-supplied next round as carried findings (`:33`, "prints its previous verdict behind the word `carried`"), and copied verbatim under `--- reviewer ---` into the approved plan (`:39`).

## What the owner sees change

Shorter reviewer output at every `READY`, and shorter approved plan files, which carry the reviewer block. Failing checks read as they do now. Passing checks read as one clause. Check (b) names citations that did not resolve and counts the ones that did. Arbiter prints the `ADMITTED` token alone.

Gate tokens, verdicts, repairs and escalations unchanged.

## Which files get touched, and roughly how

`agents/prosecutor.md:103` — PASS shape capped: `a  PASS  <the escape taken, one clause>`. Cap sits in the format block the reviewer prints from, not in prose elsewhere, so it binds where it is read.

`agents/prosecutor.md:104` — placeholder kept whole, count appended: `b  FAIL  <the claim, the citation it needed, and what the cited line actually says>; resolved: <N>`, and `b  PASS  resolved: <N>` where nothing failed. Count rides on the check's own line rather than a line of its own, because `:100` requires one line per check with every letter present and a second `b` line breaks that invariant.

`agents/prosecutor.md:53` — check (b) gains a printing rule on its citation path alone: name each citation that did not resolve, count the ones that did. Its two other failure paths, the `discovery:` header line and a misused `ASSUMED` tag, print as they do now. What the reviewer opens is untouched.

`agents/arbiter.md:197` — line becomes `N  ADMITTED`, no fields. `agents/arbiter.md:179` gains one clause saying the verdict prints as the token alone, the fields being the block's. `AMENDED` (`:198`) and `STRICKEN` (`:199`) stay: each carries a sentence that exists in no other artifact.

`docs/approved-specs.md:43` — `1  ADMITTED  <input> -> <outcome>; ...`, inside the example approved file, becomes `1  ADMITTED`.

`CHANGELOG.md:5` — one `### Changed` entry under `[Unreleased]`. The two agent definitions are kit a consumer installs, so what those reviewers print is consumer-visible.

## What it costs

`gauntlet/reviews/triviajudge-extract.plan.1.txt` is 15,764 bytes carrying five `FAIL`s; the two byte figures under **What is wrong** are about 22% of it, the chain's least favourable round shape. A round that mostly passes cuts more, since PASS prose is the whole of it.

The recital was the visible evidence of which lines the reviewer opened. `resolved: <N>` replaces an unverifiable list with an unverifiable number. The exposure is not new — no reader ever re-resolved the list — but it is cheaper to fake.

Rounds already on disk keep the old shape. No migration and no rewrite of `gauntlet/reviews/` or `gauntlet/plans/approved/`.

Out of scope: the notes at `agents/prosecutor.md:111`, the rejection format at `:113`, the arbiter's `AMENDED` and `STRICKEN` lines at `agents/arbiter.md:198-199`, its stub summary and `survives:` line at `:202-211`, and its contempt format at `:213`. Check (b)'s FAIL text keeps every part it has — see **Which files get touched**.

Owner's words draw that line: "FAIL and repair text stay full", and "Quality unchanged: findings that drive repair untouched".

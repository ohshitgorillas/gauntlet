## What the owner sees change

A `kind:` block may carry a `collateral:` section under its behavior lines, one row per broken test:

```
collateral:
- <tests dir>/<file>::<test>
  breaks: <the fact outside <tests dir>/ that moved, and what it forces>
  assertion: <the assertion, quoted verbatim, byte-identical after>
```

The target is always a test. A helper, a fixture, a parametrize list, an import: named in `breaks:`, repaired by the writer, never a target — the row's promise is about the named test's own body. So `junk-run-rule` carries two rows, one per test its three helpers feed.

A respec whose diff touches rows alone: one `arbiter` round, no second owner word.

## What it costs

Fourteen paths, one of them a directory; eight are prose. `check-gates.sh` runs `ruff`, `black`, `mypy`, `shellcheck`, the suite under `coverage` and every `--self-test`. `strike-diff.py --self-test` grows, and its docstring count moves with it (`scripts/strike-diff.py:253`). `lanes.py --self-test` does not: `hooks/lanes_selftest.py` pins no `_TESTS_LANE` prose, so item 10 changes a denial message and no pinned line.

The change's own tests go through the tests lane, so it pays a spec block and a `scrivener` round of this repo's own chain before it lands.

The shortcut's price is the owner's sight of a respec round. A collateral-only respec lands on one `arbiter` round, so the mechanical diff-shape check is the whole guard: it admits the shortcut only on a block it parted into structure line, `brief:`, behavior lines and rows, and refuses otherwise. A behavior line that changes in the same respec takes the full price, owner's word included.

Left out: no lane hook and no `PreToolUse` matcher judges a row — the `arbiter` judges it and the merge check holds it, which is where every other block field is judged. `motion: rehome` is untouched.

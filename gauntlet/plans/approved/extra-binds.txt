## What is wrong

Writable set is fixed in hook code. `hooks/bwrap-wrap.py:42` — "Writable: the repository, the session's own `/tmp`, and `~/.cache`." No project key adds a writable path.

Readable is not the gap. `hooks/bwrap-wrap.py:275-277` emits `--ro-bind / /` in every profile, so every path outside the two masks is already readable at its own spelling. A read-only extra bind ships nothing a shell can observe.

One project-controlled bind exists and is not this. `hooks/bwrap-wrap.py:366-371` binds the paths of a declared unwrapped command, read-only, reachable only by declaring a command text under `unwrapped_commands`. A project whose package cache, build cache or scratch tree sits outside the checkout declares a command it does not want unwrapped, or writes nowhere.

Declaration carries no bind key. `hooks/lane_config.py:3` — "carries eight keys, and they are the whole of what varies".

Both files the key needs are frozen at their length. `scripts/gates/file-length.py:54-61` gives `hooks/lane_config.py` 498 and `hooks/bwrap-wrap.py` 417, which are their measured lengths, and `scripts/gates/file-length.py:26` — "Nothing here raises an allowance." So neither file takes a line until it is split.

## What it costs

A writable path outside every checkout is in no lane, so no lane hook guards it. Lane hooks judge a write by its lane directory. The sandbox is the only thing holding that path today, and this key opens it by the project's own word — the price of the brief's "arbitrary paths".

`hooks/lane_config.py:446` — "disagree about the same directory, and a shell reaches the lane the write" — is the failure the middle drop exists to stop, and an entry over a mask, over `/dev`, over `/proc` or over `~/.gitconfig` is the same failure outside a lane.
